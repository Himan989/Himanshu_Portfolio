Your application question 5

How I would network 100 players across 1 km²

Server-authoritative, headless Unity 6, with Fish-Net over Netcode for GameObjects — its interest management and prediction are more mature at this player count. The client is a renderer with opinions; it never owns state.

The whole problem is that a player only needs to know about a small slice of a 1 km² map. So the map is a spatial grid of 64 m cells, and each player subscribes to their own cell plus its eight neighbours — a 192 m box of interest.

  • Tick ratesServer simulates at 30 Hz, sends at 15–20 Hz. Clients interpolate on a ~100 ms buffer; the local player predicts and reconciles.
  • On the wireDelta-compressed snapshots against each client's last acknowledged state. Positions quantised to 16 bits per axis within a cell, rotations smallest-three. Roughly 20 bytes per entity per update, not a full transform.
  • PriorityA per-client byte budget with a priority accumulator — near, visible and recently-changed entities get the packet first; distant ones degrade to a slower refresh instead of being dropped.
  • PersistenceInventory, money and XP are server-owned rows, not networked objects. Postgres of record with Redis write-behind; event-driven saves on every transfer plus a periodic flush. Item moves are idempotent transactions so a mid-trade disconnect cannot duplicate a gun.
  • Physical itemsWorld items are server entities with an ownership token. Picking up, carrying and stealing are all server-validated state transitions — the client only ever sends intent.
  • MachinesProduction does not tick. Each machine stores lastEvaluated and resolves output lazily from elapsed time when touched or when it enters someone's AOI. A thousand machines cost nothing while nobody is looking at them.
  • VehiclesServer-authoritative physics, prediction for the driver only; passengers interpolate. Fuel, damage and trunk contents are persisted records, not physics state.
  • Anti-cheatNothing client-trusted, rate limits on every intent, and server-side sanity checks on movement deltas. Habit from real-money slots, where an exploit is a chargeback.
